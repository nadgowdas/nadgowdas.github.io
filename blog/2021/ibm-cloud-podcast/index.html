IBM Cloud Podcast on "What is DevSecOps and Software Supply Chain Security ?"

September 16, 2021 · 1 min read

2021

In this recent IBM Cloud podcast, I had a great time talking to Dan around the topic that is near and dear to my heart, i.e. DevSecOps and Supply Chain Security. As I mentioned in this talk, Supply Chain Security is becoming important because our reliance on the open-source ecosystem is growing. But, in no-way I am suggesting we should avoid use of open-source software. OSS is must to drive innovation and to build our new technologies. But, open-source does not means its “free”.
