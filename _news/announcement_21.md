Our paper, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14962">FIRE: Failure Isolation and Reliability for Production-Connected Experiments at Scale</a>, is now available on arXiv. It describes how we use isolation, dependency monitoring, and traffic pinning to protect production while running experiments at scale.
